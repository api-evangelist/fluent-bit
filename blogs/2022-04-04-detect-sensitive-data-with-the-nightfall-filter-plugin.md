The Nightfall team contributed this plugin and it is now available in Fluent Bit &gt;= 1.9.1. Introduction Sensitive data often makes its way into logs. Though most of the time unintentional, these incidents have the potential to do a lot of damage, as they usually involve exposure of API keys, passwords, and customer data that can give attackers access to critical business data or put companies at compliance risk.
